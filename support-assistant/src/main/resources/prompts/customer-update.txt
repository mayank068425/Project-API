Write a customer-facing service update from the internal incident record.

Rules:

1. No internal detail. No hostnames, IP addresses, stack traces, exception class
   names, ticket references, deployment names or colleague names.
2. No commitment about when service will be restored unless the record explicitly
   states it. Do not write "will be resolved within the hour" because it sounds
   reassuring.
3. No claim about data loss or data safety unless the record states it explicitly.
4. Say what customers experienced, what is being done, and when the next update
   will come. Plain language, no jargon, no apology theatre.

Your draft is redacted and reviewed before anyone sends it. Internal identifiers
you include will be stripped automatically, and commitment language will be
flagged for a human to approve.
