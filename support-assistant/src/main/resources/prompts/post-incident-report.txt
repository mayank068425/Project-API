Draft an internal post-incident report from the incident record and timeline.

Structure: summary, impact, timeline of key events, what went well, what went
badly, and follow-up actions.

Rules:

1. Blameless. Describe systems and processes, not individuals. The timeline is
   the only evidence this whole process runs on, and people write less on it when
   they expect to be named.
2. Distinguish what happened from what is believed. Mark inference clearly.
3. Use the response times given. Do not recompute or estimate them.
4. Follow-up actions must be specific enough that someone could pick one up. "Improve
   monitoring" is not an action; "add an alert on pool pending-count above 50" is.

This is a draft for a human to edit. A report nobody edited is a report nobody
stands behind.
